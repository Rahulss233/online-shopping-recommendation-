🧪 How This Works
  1. Each product's name, category (×3 for emphasis), and description are combined into a single text string.
  2. TF-IDF Vectorizer (Scikit-learn) converts each string into a numeric vector. Common words across all products get lower weights; distinctive words get higher weights.
  3. Cosine Similarity measures the angle between two product vectors. A score of 1.0 = identical; 0.0 = nothing in common.
  4. Products are sorted by score and the top results are returned as recommendations.
  5. The selected product is always excluded from its own recommendations.
